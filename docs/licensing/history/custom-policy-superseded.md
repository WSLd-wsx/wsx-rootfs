## 1. Covered work and exclusions

"Covered Work" means only original WSX expression owned or validly controlled by
the Licensor and expressly designated in an approved, versioned coverage manifest.
No coverage manifest has yet been activated. Directory location, Git authorship,
a hash match or an account name alone does not establish coverage or ownership.

Third-party software, upstream patch context, GPL-covered combined works, existing
MIT/Apache/CC0 grants, and rights already validly granted are excluded. Original
additions to upstream works require individual compatibility review. A repository
root, rootfs image or compiled compositor is not covered wholesale. Existing
copyright and license notices must remain intact. Nothing here narrows rights
available independently under another applicable license.

## 2. Proposed noncommercial grant

Subject to these terms, the proposed grant would allow individuals to use,
reproduce, study, debug and modify Covered Work for personal noncommercial use,
and to redistribute its source under Section 4. No automatic conversion to an
open-source license is proposed. No binary redistribution permission is offered.

"Commercial Use" would include incorporating Covered Work into commercial
products, paid or customer-facing services, or an organization's actual internal
operational deployment, whether or not copies are sold or that deployment earns
a direct profit. It would require a separate written authorization from the
Licensor. Organizational nonproduction evaluation is addressed in Section 3.

Nonprofit, educational, donation-supported and consulting arrangements require
an explicit final definition before activation; they must not be silently treated
as either permitted or prohibited merely by an organization's label.

## 3. Enterprise evaluation

Confirmed policy: organizations may evaluate Covered Work for 30 days in a
nonproduction environment. At expiry they must stop covered use or obtain a
commercial authorization. Evaluation does not authorize actual internal operations,
production workloads or customer services.

Proposed mechanics awaiting approval: count consecutive calendar days from the
organization's first evaluation use, with no reset through reinstalls, personnel
changes or a new copy. After expiry, permit retention of inactive archival copies
and evaluation reports, but no further execution except under a new authorization.
No automatic deletion or destruction of data is required by this draft.

## 4. Modification and source redistribution

Confirmed policy: noncommercial modification and source redistribution may be
allowed if notices and the applicable restrictions are preserved. Proposed
mechanics: provide these terms and the coverage manifest with each source copy,
identify modifications and their dates, and preserve all applicable third-party
notices. Recipients obtain only the same covered permissions; no distributor may
grant commercial rights on the Licensor's behalf.

Binary redistribution, including embedding Covered Work in an image, container,
installer or executable offered to others, remains a separate owner decision.
It is not permitted by this proposed grant alone. This restriction concerns only
Covered Work and does not remove independent redistribution rights in exclusions.

## 5. Separate authorization and contributions

Commercial permission must be obtained separately from wgy@wsxdesk.com.
Payment, a donation, public source access or possession of a build does not itself
constitute commercial authorization. No trademark endorsement is granted.

Submitting a contribution does not automatically assign copyright to WGY or
establish permission to relicense it commercially. Contributions need a documented
inbound-rights policy before acceptance into the covered scope. Patent permission
is not settled by this draft and must be resolved explicitly before activation.

## 6. Proposed warranty, termination and preservation clauses

To the extent allowed by applicable law, Covered Work would be supplied "AS IS",
without warranties of merchantability, fitness or noninfringement; liability
limitations must be reviewed for the applicable jurisdiction before activation.
Mandatory rights and liabilities cannot be excluded by this paragraph.

Proposed termination rule: permissions for Covered Work cease upon breach;
commercial permissions require a separate agreement. Cure periods, reinstatement
and treatment of downstream recipients remain explicit decisions, not assumed
terms. Termination cannot revoke independent third-party or previously granted
rights. No clause purports to erase earlier copies, grants, forks or caches.
